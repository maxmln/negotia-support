Negotia

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Negotia is an iPhone app for learning negotiation and persuasion skills. It is made by Rollbase Ltd EOOD. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends anything off your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, quiz scores, practice history and activity days are saved on your iPhone only, in the app's private storage. We never receive them. They are included in your iPhone backups like any other app data. Deleting the app, or using Reset Progress in the Progress tab, removes them.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing. The app does not use the network and contains no third-party code.

What we don't do

We don't collect personal information, don't sell or share data, don't track you across apps or websites and don't build profiles. Negotia is not directed at children under 13.
