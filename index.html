Negotia

Negotiation skills for iPhone: short lessons, live practice conversations and daily drills.

Support

Found a mistake in a lesson, or something that doesn't work? Email maxbogdanoff@gmail.com or open an issue and describe what happened. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your progress is saved on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything in the app works without a connection.

How do I start over?

Progress tab → Reset Progress.

How is a topic mastered?

Score 80% or more on a topic's quiz. You can retake any quiz.

What does Negotia cost?

Nothing. There are no ads and no purchases.
